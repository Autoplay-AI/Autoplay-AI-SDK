<Note>
  **Coming soon.** Proactive triggers for Maven — reaching out automatically based on what a user is doing in your product (a stuck flow, a repeated error, a high-intent moment) — are on the way.
</Note>

Once **[Step 1 — Connect real-time events](/recipes/maven/step-1-connect-real-time-events)** is live, Maven can already *pull* a user's recent activity on demand. Proactive triggers add the other direction: acting on that activity automatically, without waiting for the user to ask.

Want early access or have a use case in mind? Join our [Discord](https://discord.gg/jCbR2tQA5) and let us know.
