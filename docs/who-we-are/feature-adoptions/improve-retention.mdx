## Outcome

Users who hit friction or cancellation intent get timely, personalized guidance instead of silently leaving.

## How Autoplay helps

- **Activity-aware triggers** — detect high-signal actions (e.g. billing page visits, repeated errors) and fire [proactive offers](/sdk/proactive-triggers-builtins).
- **Visual guidance** — route qualifying outcomes into in-app tours via your delivery channel. See Step 4 of the [proactive copilot guide](/sdk/proactive-copilot/step-4-enhance-with-user-memory).
- **User memory** — track workflow completion across sessions so nudges stay relevant. See [User memory](/sdk/user-memory).

## Get started

Define triggers in [Proactive triggers authoring](/sdk/proactive-triggers-authoring) and wire delivery through your chatbot integration.
