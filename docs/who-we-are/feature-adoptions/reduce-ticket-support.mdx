## Outcome

Fewer tickets reach your human queue because users get the right answer — or visual walkthrough — while they're still in the product.

## How Autoplay helps

- **Real-time context** — your agent sees clicks, navigation, and stuck patterns via [typed payloads](/sdk/typed-payloads).
- **Proactive triggers** — surface help when frustration signals appear, before a ticket is filed. See [Proactive triggers](/sdk/proactive-triggers).
- **Agent session states** — cooldown and timeout gating so proactive nudges don't feel spammy. See [Agent session states](/sdk/agent-states).
