## Outcome

More feature adoption and expansion revenue by nudging users toward capabilities they're already close to needing.

## How Autoplay helps

- **Golden paths & workflows** — know which steps a user has completed and what's next. See [User memory](/sdk/user-memory) and Step 5 of the [proactive copilot guide](/sdk/proactive-copilot/step-5-enrich-with-user-memory).
- **Proactive triggers** — fire contextual offers when users explore adjacent features or hit usage limits. See [Built-in triggers](/sdk/proactive-triggers-builtins).
- **Visual tours** — show the feature in-app instead of describing it in chat alone.

## Get started

Record workflows in the Autoplay dashboard, then wire [proactive triggers](/sdk/proactive-triggers) to your chatbot or tour provider.
