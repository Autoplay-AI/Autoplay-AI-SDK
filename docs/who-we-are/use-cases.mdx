Autoplay fits anywhere you want an AI agent to understand **what users are doing in your product** and act before they ask for help.

## Outcome-focused use cases

Pick the outcome you're optimizing for:

- [Reduce ticket support](/who-we-are/feature-adoptions/reduce-ticket-support) — deflect repetitive tickets with proactive, context-aware help
- [Improve retention](/who-we-are/feature-adoptions/improve-retention) — catch at-risk users and guide them back to value
- [Improve CSAT](/who-we-are/feature-adoptions/improve-csat) — resolve issues faster with agents that already know what users tried
- [Upsell features](/who-we-are/feature-adoptions/upsell-features) — surface the right feature at the right moment based on live product activity
